- President Seguro named on screen only, not in the narration.
- The narration below as written (approved 16 Oct 2026).

**Core message:** Portuguese citizens from 18 (also abroad) and Brazilians with the equal-rights status vote with one
cross for a party (no names on the ballot). 22 districts share 230 seats by D'Hondt, no threshold. One chamber: the
Assembly makes the laws and can bring down the government. In 2025 the Democratic Alliance won 91 seats (116 = majority);
no vote is needed to take office: the President appoints the PM, who governs until a majority votes him out (only 10
voted to reject his programme). The directly elected President can veto laws and dissolve parliament: that is how
Portugal got its 2022, 2024 and 2025 elections.

## Fact sheet (checked 16 Oct 2026)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| **Who can vote:** Portuguese citizens 18+ on the voter roll (automatic with the citizen card), in Portugal or abroad; Brazilians resident in Portugal with the equal-political-rights status (granted after 3 years' habitual residence) vote in Assembly elections. Not compulsory. | [CNE FAQ: right to vote](https://www.cne.pt/faq2/95/3), [Portal do Eleitor: foreign residents](https://www.portaldoeleitor.pt/pt/Faqs/ER/Pages/default.aspx), [Portal do Eleitor: registration](https://www.portaldoeleitor.pt/pt/Faqs/RE/Pages/default.aspx) |
| **Abroad:** choose postal or in-person voting at consulates; no choice = post; two constituencies (Europe, outside Europe), 2 MPs each. | [CNE FAQ: voting abroad](https://www.cne.pt/faq2/113/3), [Portal do Eleitor: residents abroad](https://www.portaldoeleitor.pt/Paginas/Residentes-estrangeiro.aspx) |
| **Early voting in mobility:** any voter registered in Portugal, sign up online, vote the Sunday before in any municipality (2025: sign-up 4–8 May, vote 11 May). | [CNE: 2025 early voting leaflet](https://www.cne.pt/sites/default/files/dl/eleicoes/2025_ar/voto_antecipado/2025_ar_folheto_voto_antecipado_mobilidade.pdf), [Portal do Eleitor](https://www.portaldoeleitor.pt/pt/Noticias/Pages/Voto-Antecipado-em-Mobilidade-2025.aspx) |
| **Ballot:** party names, acronyms and symbols with an empty square each; closed lists, no candidate names; one cross; more than one square marked, or drawings/words = void. | [Electoral law (Lei 14/79)](https://www.portaldoeleitor.pt/pt/Eleicao/Legislacao/Documents/Legisla%C3%A7%C3%A3o/Doc_Lei_AR.pdf), [CNE: how to vote 2025](https://www.cne.pt/sites/default/files/dl/eleicoes/2025_ar/docs_geral/2025_ar-folheto_acessivel-modo-de-votar.pdf) |
| **Seats:** 230 MPs; 226 in national districts by population, 2 per constituency abroad; D'Hondt; no legal threshold. 2025: Lisbon 48, Portalegre 2. | [Electoral law](https://www.cne.pt/sites/default/files/dl/legis_lear_consolidada_2019_02_15.pdf), [Mapa Oficial 2-A/2025](https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2025/05/104a00/0000200016.pdf) |
| **Election 18 May 2025:** AD 91, Chega 60, PS 58, IL 9, Livre 6, CDU 3, BE 1, PAN 1, JPP 1 = 230. | [Parlamento: election results](https://www.parlamento.pt/sites/EN/Parliament/Paginas/Election-results.aspx), [IPU Parline](https://data.ipu.org/parliament/PT/PT-LC01/election/PT-LC01-E20250518/), [Mapa Oficial](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/mapa-oficial/2025-924052233) |
| **Government:** PM appointed by the President after hearing the parties, "taking the election results into account"; the programme falls only if a rejection motion gets an absolute majority (116). Montenegro appointed 29 May 2025, sworn in 5 June; PCP motion rejected 18 June 2025 (for: PCP, Livre, BE = 10; PAN abstained). | [Presidency: PM appointed](https://marcelorebelodesousa.arquivo.presidencia.pt/atualidade/toda-a-atualidade/2025/05/presidente-da-republica-indigita-primeiro-ministro/), [Parlamento: motions](https://www.parlamento.pt/ArquivoDocumentacao/Documents/Folha_Mocoes.pdf), [DN: programme approved](https://www.dn.pt/política/programa-do-governo-em-debate-pelo-segundo-dia-com-aprovação-garantida) |
| **Dissolutions:** 2025: government lost a confidence vote (11 Mar), President dissolved (decree 31-A/2025). 2024: after PM Costa resigned. 2022: after the 2022 budget was voted down. | [Presidency: dissolution 2025](https://marcelorebelodesousa.arquivo.presidencia.pt/atualidade/toda-a-atualidade/2025/03/presidente-da-republica-dissolve-parlamento-e-convoca-eleicoes/), [Parlamento: dissolution](https://www.parlamento.pt/Paginas/2025/marco/A-Atividade-Parlamentar-no-periodo-da-dissolucao-da-AR.aspx) |
