# "Xi won 2,952 votes to zero." — China's election system (ChineseElection)

Made 5 Nov 2026 under the standing brief ("educational motion graphics video, political system") and the TikTok
Retention Rules, with no questions asked (the user's standing instruction). Request: "Make a motion graphics video
about the Chinese election system. Same style as the Russian one" → the style of `RussianElection2036` (the current
standard look), themed in China's flag colours.

## Choices made (no questions)
- **Look:** the `RussianElection2036` / `UkrainianElection` standard look in **deep Chinese red** with **yellow**
  accent blocks (red letters, thin white rim), **yellow** full-screen flashes for the two re-hooks, cream paper
  scenes for the ballot and the 2023 result, the broadcast frame with no small text, huge outlined section numerals.
- **Subtitle pill:** red is in the flag but does not read on the red background, so a **yellow pill with dark red
  text** (dark words with a red pill and yellow text on the yellow flashes; dark words with a red pill on paper).
- **Hook:** "Xi won 2,952 votes to zero." (result first, the strongest fact, works on mute).
- **Story:** Vote → the ladder of congresses → the National People's Congress → President and Premier → the Party,
  told as six numbered sections. China has one chamber, so there is no upper house step.
- **Rewatch detail:** a small yellow star floating above the pyramid on frame 0 (it looks like decoration, as the
  flag has stars). Explained at the end: it is the Communist Party, above the whole ladder.
- **Who can vote:** citizens from 18 (unless a court removed their political rights), but only directly for the
  township and county congresses (3.8 s, counted in the background budget).
- **Timely angle:** a new five-yearly round of county and township elections is under way in 2026 ("this year, a
  billion people vote").
- **Save line:** "Save this for 2027" (the next Party Congress, which picks the leadership, is due in 2027).
- **Closing question:** "Is one name on a ballot a real choice?" (YES/NO), in the hook's layout.
- **Neutral tone:** only official rules and official vote counts; "the real boss" is the party leader, which is how
  official and outside sources both describe the general secretary's role. No likeness of real people.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `bm_george`, en-gb, speed 1.25 (raised from 1.22 to fit). "Xi" read with `pronounce` (ʃˈiː).
- **Length:** first draft 65.1 s → trimmed the premier, result, save and star lines and sped up slightly →
  **57.5 s of voice + 1.2 s hold = 58.7 s**.
