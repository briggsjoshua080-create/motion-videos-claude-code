# Retention plan: "The president just failed his midterm." (1080×1920, 30 fps, 59.95 s)

Made to the **TikTok Retention Rules** in `CLAUDE.md` and the **comedy formula**
(`briefs/comedy-explainer-formula.md`). Second marks are **measured** from the voice-over
(`src/voiceover/us-midterms.timing.json`: last word at 58.75 s, then a 1.2 s hold). No word-for-word script here;
the narration lines live in `src/voiceover/us-midterms.script.json`.

Loops: **L1** what midterm? · **L2** who grades him? · **L3** why do they almost always fail? · **L4** (re-hook 1)
why would the whole House reapply? · **L5** (re-hook 2) what else is on that ballot? · **L6** (hidden) the horse
sticker on the report card.

**Voice gist** (not a script): the president failed his midterm → what, who, why → halfway through the term America
elects Congress; lose seats, fail → since WWII only two presidents passed → "the weird part": the whole House
reapplies every two years; senators get six → Congress writes the laws and controls the money → graders: citizens
from 18 who registered (North Dakota skips that) → "now it gets strange": Americans even elect sheriffs → one ballot,
one oval per race → most votes wins a district, 218 wins the House → on Tuesday Democrats won both → from January
every law needs both parties ("Hello, gridlock") → angry voters show up, happy ones stay on the couch → send this to
someone who skipped it → the horse: 1845 farmers rode to vote, never on Sundays, hence Tuesday → should America scrap
midterms?
