## Pinned comment
For the "scrap them" crowd: a president actually tried. In January 1966 LBJ asked Congress for four-year House terms,
which would have ended midterms. It never passed, and that November his party lost 47 House seats. My take: two-year
terms keep the House on a short leash, but they also mean members start campaigning the day they're sworn in. Is a
permanent campaign the price of accountability, or just the price of chaos? 👇
