**The first video made to the TikTok Retention Rules** (`CLAUDE.md`, added 3 Nov 2026) and, at the user's request,
**the new standard** that future videos copy. The beat-by-beat plan with second marks, the rule check and the scores
are in `retention-plan.md`; the A/B hooks, caption and pinned comment in `posting.md`. There is no word-for-word
script here (the user's rule); the narration lines live only in `src/voiceover/ukrainian-election.script.json`.

**Style:** the default look (`SwedishElection`, the user's Belgian reference) in **Ukraine's flag colours**, adapted to
the rules: a strong deep-blue background (`#0B4EA6`, lighter in the middle, darker at the edges) with white text;
accent words in **yellow blocks with dark blue letters** (`#FFD700` / `#0A2552`); a **full yellow flash for each
re-hook**; light paper scenes for the two ballots; the broadcast frame without small text (corner marks, ruler, beat
dots); numbered sections as huge outlined numerals; **no title card** (frame 0 is the hook line) and a last frame that
mirrors it. **Subtitle pill: yellow with dark blue text** (red is not in the flag), at **110 px**, switching to dark
blue words with a dark blue pill while the background is paper or yellow.

**No questions asked** (the user's standing instruction). Choices made:
- Vertical 1080×1920 @ 30 fps, 59.8 s (58.6 s of voice + a 1.2 s hold): inside the 45–60 s target. AI narrator
  (Kokoro `bm_george`, en-gb, speed 1.2, pauses 0.16–0.45 s). First take 59.5 s of voice; sped up from 1.18 to 1.2

  caption stays up 2 s (rule 6).
- **Hook:** "Elections are banned in Ukraine." (true under martial law; explained at 10–13 s). Alternatives in `posting.md`.
- **Story:** the ban → who is in charge and how they got there → what changes when voting returns, told as numbered
  sections 01 The ban, 02 The vote (who can vote, the presidential ballot, the 2019 result), 03 Parliament,
  04 Government, 05 Next vote, 06 the rewatch payoff, then the question.
- **Worked example:** the most recent national elections are the **2019 presidential election** (Zelensky) and the
  **2019 snap Rada election** (Servant of the People alone); the government shown is the current one (PM Koretskyi,
  July 2026). The Rada ballot shown is the one in the **2019 Electoral Code** (never used for the Rada yet), not the
  2019 mixed system, because that is what voters will get next time.
- **Rewatch detail:** the 26 seats nobody could vote for in 2019 (occupied districts) are drawn as hollow grey rings
  at the right end of the hemicycle at 35–40 s and only explained at 52–56 s.
- **Left out for length** (in the fact sheet): the veto and its two-thirds override, the president's power to dissolve
  the Rada, the 2019 mixed system (half list, half districts), the top-nine national list and the 25%-of-quota rule for
  preference votes, the CEC's six-month preparation request, the Rada renewing martial law every 90 days, Zelensky's
  60-day-ceasefire offer and the post-war election law still being drafted.
- **Neutrality:** the video states the law and the results; the debate is only in the closing question. Zelensky,
  Koretskyi and Servant of the People are named in text and voice only (no likeness; generic outlines). The parties
  on both ballots, the vote bars and the 5% chart are made up.
- **Closing question:** "Should Ukraine vote before the war ends?" (YES/NO), after "Save this for election day."

## Fact sheet (checked 3 Nov 2026)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| **Elections banned:** martial law since 24 Feb 2022; the Law on the legal regime of martial law (art. 19) bans presidential, parliamentary and local elections while it lasts. The Constitution keeps the Rada (art. 83) and the president (art. 108) in office until their successors take over. Rada resolution of 25 Feb 2025 (268 votes): no elections under martial law, Zelensky serves until a new president takes office. | [OPORA: why there can be no presidential election under martial law](https://oporaua.org/viyna/chomu-viboriv-prezidenta-do-kincya-voyennogo-stanu-ne-mozhe-buti-25161), [OPORA: martial law and the powers of state bodies](https://oporaua.org/vybory/vpliv-voyennogo-stanu-na-provedennya-viboriv-i-povnovazhnist-organiv-vladi-25059), [Al Jazeera, 25 Feb 2025](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/2/25/ukrainian-parliament-affirms-zelenskyys-legitimacy) |
| **Latest extension:** the Rada extended martial law for the **21st** time on 26 Oct 2026, to **29 Jan 2027** (293 votes), on Zelensky's proposal. | [sud.ua](https://sud.ua/en/news/laws/375884-verkhovna-rada-proholosuvala-za-prodovzhennia-voiennoho-stanu-do-29-sichnia-2027-roku), [Anadolu](https://aa.com.tr/en/world/ukraine-extends-martial-law-until-january-2027/4101179) |
| **Who can vote:** citizens who are 18 on election day, except people a court has declared legally incapable (Constitution art. 70); no sign-up (State Register of Voters); voting abroad at polling stations in embassies and consulates (101 in 72 countries in 2019, none in Russia); internally displaced people can move their voting address (CEC resolution 88); the Constitutional Court struck down a ban on prisoners voting. Not compulsory. | [Constitution (Rada, EN)](https://zakonst.rada.gov.ua/docs/en/constitution.pdf), [Election Code (CEC, EN)](https://www.cvk.gov.ua/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Election-Code-of-Ukraine.pdf), [Ukrinform: 101 polling stations abroad](https://www.ukrinform.net/amp/rubric-elections/2658301-cec-creates-101-polling-station-commissions-abroad.html), [Constitutional Court: the right to vote](https://ccu.gov.ua/en/node/17373), [Council of Europe: IDP voting](https://www.coe.int/en/web/kyiv/-/idps-will-vote-in-the-next-local-elections-in-ukraine) |
| **President:** elected directly for 5 years (art. 103); more than half of the votes cast wins, otherwise a run-off between the top two three weeks later; the voter puts a "+" or any mark in the square next to one name (CEC). Commander-in-chief, can veto laws (two thirds of the Rada override), puts the PM forward on the coalition's proposal (art. 106, 114, 94). Candidates: 35+, 10 years' residence, the state language. | [CEC: presidential ballot and voting](https://cvk.gov.ua/actualna-informaciya/do-uvagi-sub-iektiv-viborchih-protsesiv-4.html), [Constitution (Rada, EN)](https://zakonst.rada.gov.ua/docs/en/constitution.pdf), [OPORA: candidates in autumn 2026](https://oporaua.org/vybory/vibori-voseni-2026-roku-pravovi-peredumovi-ta-mozhlivist-uchasti-kandidativ-26129) |
| **2019 presidential election:** 39 candidates; round one 31 Mar: Zelensky 30.24%, Poroshenko 15.95%; run-off 21 Apr: **Zelensky 73.22%**, Poroshenko 24.45% (CEC), the highest share in Ukraine's presidential elections (Chesno). Took office 20 May 2019. | [Ukrinform: CEC result](https://www.ukrinform.ua/rubric-elections/2691466-prezidentom-ukraini-stav-volodimir-zelenskij.html), [Ukrinform: round one](https://ukrinform.net/rubric-elections/2676150-cec-announces-results-of-first-round-of-presidential-elections.html), [Gazeta.ua / Chesno](https://gazeta.ua/ru/articles/politics/_zelenskij-pobil-rekord-kravchuka-infografika/899102) |
| **2019 Rada election (21 Jul, snap):** Servant of the People, named after Zelensky's TV series, 43.16% of the list vote, **254 seats** (124 list + 130 districts), the first single-party majority in the Rada's history; 5 parties over 5%. Only 424 of 450 seats filled: the vote could not be held in **26 single-member districts** in occupied territory (Crimea and Sevastopol 12, Donetsk oblast 9, Luhansk oblast 5). | [OSCE/ODIHR final report](https://www.osce.org/sites/default/files/f/documents/6/9/439634_0.pdf), [CEC analytical report 2019](https://www.cvk.gov.ua/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/2019-NDU-en-Analytical-report.pdf), [NV: final count](https://nv.ua/ukr/amp/vibori-2019-ostatochni-rezultati-50034203.html) |
| **The Rada:** 450 MPs, five-year term, one chamber; makes laws, passes the budget, appoints the PM and cabinet (226 votes), approves martial law every 90 days. | [Constitution (Rada, EN)](https://zakonst.rada.gov.ua/docs/en/constitution.pdf) |
| **Government now:** PM Yulia Svyrydenko resigned (Rada accepted 14 Jul 2026); **Serhii Koretskyi** (ex-head of Naftogaz) appointed PM on **16 Jul 2026** with 289 votes (resolution 4941-IX). Denys Shmyhal is first deputy PM and energy minister. | [Rada](https://www.rada.gov.ua/news/Novyny/274296.html), [Cabinet of Ministers](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/verkhovna-rada-pryznachyla-serhiia-koretskoho-premier-ministrom-ukrainy), [zakon.rada.gov.ua 4941-IX](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/4941-20), [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/07/16/8044351/) |
| **Next Rada vote (Electoral Code of 19 Dec 2019):** fully proportional, 450 seats, **5% national threshold**, open regional lists in 27 regions. One ballot: a square next to each party and a "№" box to write in the number of one candidate from that party's regional list (art. 168); a candidate vote also counts for the party; the top nine of each national list are fixed; candidates with at least 25% of the quota move up the regional list (art. 185 wording varies by edition). Used for big local councils in 2020, never for the Rada. | [CEC: Election Code (EN)](https://www.cvk.gov.ua/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Election-Code-of-Ukraine.pdf), [protocol.ua: art. 168](https://protocol.ua/ua/viborchiy_kodeks_ukraini_stattya_168/), [OPORA: the new system](https://oporaua.org/en/vybory/electoral_system_2019-19546), [IFES FAQ 2020 local elections](https://www.ifes.org/sites/default/files/migrate/ifes_faqs_elections_in_ukraine_2020_local_elections_october_2020.pdf) |
| **When voting returns:** elections must be called within a month after martial law ends (90-day presidential and 60-day parliamentary campaigns); the CEC asked (Jan 2026) for a six-month preparation period first. A cross-party Rada working group is drafting a one-off post-war election law (voting abroad, soldiers, displaced people, occupied areas); no bill registered by Jul 2026. Zelensky (Oct 2026): elections if there is a 60-day full ceasefire; the Kremlin rejected it. US-mediated talks are planned for 10–11 Nov 2026. | [Interfax-Ukraine: CEC six months](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1138354.html), [OPORA: legal note on post-war election dates](https://oporaua.org/vybory/yuridichna-dovidka-pro-stroki-provedennya-zagal-noderzhavnih-povoyennih-viboriv-25636), [Rada: working group](https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/News/top_news/269633.html), [Ukrinform, 12 Oct 2026](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-polytics/4173570-zelensky-ready-to-work-with-rada-on-election-solutions-if-fighting-stops-for-60-days.html), [UNITED24](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/kremlin-rejects-zelenskyys-60-day-ceasefire-proposal-for-elections-stays-silent-on-energy-truce-23351) |
