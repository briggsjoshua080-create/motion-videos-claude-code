## Fact sheet (checked 27 Oct 2026)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| **Who can vote:** Serbian citizens who are 18 by election day and have permanent residence in Serbia (not if deprived of legal capacity by a court). Registration is passive (Unified Voter Register, Ministry of Public Administration and Local Self-Government). Voters living abroad must **ask to vote abroad** before a deadline (3 Oct 2026 for this election); they vote at embassies/consulates. 6,451,292 registered voters, 102,147 of them abroad. Since 2024 a new address counts only after 6 months. Voters registered in Kosovo voted at polling stations in Vranje, Kuršumlija, Raška and Tutin. Not compulsory. | [ODIHR preliminary statement, 26 Oct 2026](https://odihr.osce.org/sites/default/files/documents/official_documents/2026/10/SRB%20Early%20Parliamentary%202026_PS_26.10.2026.pdf), [ODIHR interim report, 9 Oct 2026](https://odihr.osce.org/sites/default/files/documents/official_documents/2026/10/SRB%20Early%20Parliamentary%202026%20Interim%20Report_09.10.2026.pdf), [RIK: parliamentary elections](https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/tekst/en/269/parliamentary-elections.php) |
| **Ballot:** one ballot with the electoral lists; you vote for a list, not a candidate, by **circling the ordinal number in front of one list**; fold it and put it in the box. Lists are closed (fixed order). **Invisible (UV) ink** is sprayed on the voter's finger, which is checked under a UV lamp, to stop double voting. | [RIK: parliamentary elections](https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/tekst/en/269/parliamentary-elections.php), [RIK: Law on the Election of MPs (2020, English)](https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/extfile/en/74/2020%20Law%20on%20the%20Election%20of%20Members%20of%20Parliament%20-%20inofficially%20consolidated%20text_12.5.2020..pdf), [ODIHR report (invisible ink)](https://cdn.osce.org/sites/default/files/f/documents/f/c/90149.pdf) |
| **Seats:** 250 MPs, four-year term, **one nationwide constituency**, closed lists, D'Hondt; **3% threshold** (lowered from 5% in 2020); **lists of national minorities are exempt** (and their quotients are raised by 35%). At least 40% of candidates must be women. | [parlament.gov.rs: elections and electoral system](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/national-assembly/role-and-mode-of-operation/elections-and-electoral-system.502.html), [IPU Parline: 2020 election](https://data.ipu.org/parliament/RS/RS-LC01/election/RS-LC01-E20200621) |
| **National Assembly:** one chamber; passes laws and the budget, elects and can dismiss the government, amends the Constitution. | [parlament.gov.rs: jurisdiction](http://www.parlament.gov.rs/national-assembly/role-and-mode-of-operation/jurisdiction.501.html), [Constitution (English)](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/documents/Constitution_%20of_Serbia_pdf.pdf) |
| **Prime minister:** the President proposes a candidate after hearing the elected lists; the Assembly elects the government with a **majority of all MPs (126 of 250)**. No government within **90 days** of the Assembly's first sitting → dissolution and new election. | [Constitution, art. 127–128 & 109](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/documents/Constitution_%20of_Serbia_pdf.pdf), [parlament.gov.rs: rules of procedure, President](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/national-assembly/important-documents/rules-of-procedure-(consolidated-text)/relations-between-the-national-assembly-and-the-president-of-republic.1364.html) |
| **President:** directly elected for **five years**, at most two terms; needs a majority of votes cast, otherwise a **run-off** of the top two within 15 days. Can **return a law** to the Assembly once; if it is passed again by a majority of all MPs, the President must promulgate it. Proposes the PM; dissolves the Assembly in the cases set by the Constitution. | [RIK: presidential elections](https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/tekst/en/275/presidential-elections.php), [predsednik.rs: the President](https://www.predsednik.rs/en/president), [Constitution, art. 113–116](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/documents/Constitution_%20of_Serbia_pdf.pdf) |
| **The hook:** Aleksandar Vučić (President since 2017, re-elected 2022) **resigned on 27 Sep 2026** to lead his list for prime minister; Speaker **Ana Brnabić is acting President** (from 28 Sep 2026, for at most three months); a presidential election is due **by late Dec 2026** (Al Jazeera: 27 Dec; no date announced yet). The Assembly was dissolved and the snap election called on 9 Sep 2026. Background: protests led by students since the Novi Sad canopy collapse (1 Nov 2024). | [srbija.gov.rs: Vučić resigns](https://www.srbija.gov.rs/vest/en/288482/president-of-republic-of-serbia-aleksandar-vucic-resigns.php), [srbija.gov.rs: duty handed to the Speaker](https://www.srbija.gov.rs/vest/en/288494/vucic-hands-over-duty-of-president-of-republic-to-national-assembly-speaker.php), [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/serbia-president-vucic-to-resign/33790653.html), [Al Jazeera: who will be PM](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/26/serbia-after-vucic-who-will-be-pm-and-when-will-a-government-be-formed) |
| **Election of 25 Oct 2026 (preliminary, RIK, 8,187 polling stations):** Student List 2,267,615 votes (52.71%) = **140 seats**; "Aleksandar Vučić – United Serbia" 1,685,022 (39.17%) = **104 seats**; four minority lists share the remaining **6**.
