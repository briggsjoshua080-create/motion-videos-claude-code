## Fact sheet (checked 28 Oct 2026)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| **Who can vote:** Slovenian citizens who are **18** on election day (since 2024 also adults under guardianship). Registration is **passive** (from the population register; separate lists for voters at home, abroad and the two national communities). Voters abroad vote **by post** (ballots go automatically to voters with no permanent residence in Slovenia; must be sent by 19:00 on election day) or at an embassy/consulate. **115,392** voters on the abroad list (6 Mar 2026). Not compulsory. | [gov.si: voters and the voting rights register](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/voters-and-the-voting-rights-register/), [DVK: election education](https://www.dvk-rs.si/en/election-education/), [ODIHR final report 2026](https://odihr.osce.org/sites/default/files/documents/official_documents/2026/07/SLO%20Parliamentary%202026_final%20report_03.07.2026.pdf), [Constitution](https://www.us-rs.si/en/legal-basis/constitution) |
| **Two votes for minorities:** members of the **Italian and Hungarian** communities who are on the community register vote in their ordinary constituency **and** in a special constituency for their community's MP (2 seats, now first past the post). Upheld by the Constitutional Court (1998); ODIHR recommends reviewing it. | [DVK: National Assembly](https://www.dvk-rs.si/en/elections/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-slovenia/), [gov.si: elections to the National Assembly](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/elections-to-the-national-assembly/), [ODIHR 2011 report](https://www.osce.org/sites/default/files/f/documents/6/b/87786.pdf) |
| **Ballot:** the ballot of your **electoral district** lists **one candidate per party**; you vote for one by **circling the number** in front of the name. The vote counts for the party's list in the constituency; the party's seats go to its candidates with the best share in their districts. | [DVK: election education](https://www.dvk-rs.si/en/election-education/), [DVK: National Assembly](https://www.dvk-rs.si/en/elections/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-slovenia/) |
| **Seats:** **90 MPs**, four-year term: **88** by proportional representation in **8 constituencies × 11 districts** (Droop quota in the constituencies, D'Hondt nationally), **4% threshold**; **2** for the Italian and Hungarian communities. Preferential voting won a non-binding referendum (9 Jun 2024, 70.9%) but has not been enacted (needs two thirds). | [gov.si: elections to the National Assembly](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/elections-to-the-national-assembly/), [DVK: National Assembly](https://www.dvk-rs.si/en/elections/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-slovenia/), [ODIHR NAM report Jan 2026](https://odihr.osce.org/sites/default/files/documents/official_documents/2026/01/SLO%20parliamentary%202025%20NAM%20report_26.01.2026.pdf) |
| **National Assembly:** the main chamber; passes laws and the budget, elects the PM and appoints the ministers. | [DZ: competences](https://www.dz-rs.si/wps/portal/en/Home/AboutNA/Competences/competences/), [gov.si: political system](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/political-system/) |
| **National Council:** **40 members**, elected **indirectly** for five years by interest groups (4 employers, 4 employees, 4 farmers/crafts/professions, 6 non-commercial fields, 22 local interests). Within 7 days it can make the Assembly **decide again** on a law (suspensive veto); the Assembly then needs a majority of all MPs (46), and that vote is final. It can also propose laws. | [ds-rs.si: general](https://ds-rs.si/en/about-national-council/general), [gov.si: National Council](https://www.gov.si/en/state-authorities/national-council/) |
| **Prime minister:** after consulting the groups, the **President proposes** a candidate; a **majority of all MPs (46 of 90)**, secret ballot. If not elected (or none proposed), **party groups or at least 10 MPs** may propose candidates within 14 days; failing that, a further round needs only a majority of votes cast, else dissolution. | [Constitution art. 111](https://www.us-rs.si/en/legal-basis/constitution), [gov.si: political system](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/political-system/) |
| **President:** directly elected, **five years**, at most two consecutive terms; a majority of valid votes, otherwise a **run-off** of the top two. Must promulgate laws within **eight days** (no veto). Proposes the PM. **Nataša Pirc Musar** in office since **23 Dec 2022** (first woman); next election due **late 2027**. | [gov.si: election of the President](https://www.gov.si/en/topics/election-of-the-president-of-the-republic/), [predsednica-slo.si: about](https://www.predsednica-slo.si/en/about-president), [gov.si: first female president](https://www.gov.si/en/news/2022-11-18-slovenia-elects-the-first-female-president-of-the-republic-in-the-countrys-history/) |
| **Election of 22 Mar 2026 (DVK, official):** Freedom Movement (Golob) **29** (28.66%), SDS (Janša) **28** (27.88%), NSi–SLS–Fokus 9 (9.26%), SD 6 (6.71%), Democrats (Logar) 6, Levica & Vesna 5, Resni.ca 5, national communities 2. Turnout **70.25%**. | [DVK results 2026](https://volitve.dvk-rs.si/dz2026/en/), [STA: results certified](https://english.sta.si/3542353/electoral-commission-officially-certifies-general-election-results), [IPU Parline](https://data.ipu.org/parliament/SI/SI-LC01/election/SI-LC01-E20260322/) |
| **Government:** Golob announced on 20 Apr 2026 that his party would go into opposition; the President **did not propose anyone** in the first round (no majority in sight); MPs nominated **Janša** (48 signatures), elected PM on **22 May 2026** by **51 votes to 36** (secret ballot); coalition SDS, NSi, SLS + Fokus, Democrats = **43 seats**, Resni.ca (5) backs it from outside (its leader Zoran Stevanović is Speaker). 16th government appointed **4 Jun 2026**. | [STA: Janša elected PM](https://english.sta.si/3558529/parliament-elects-jansa-prime-minister), [STA: President will not nominate](https://english.sta.si/3549156/president-will-not-nominate-pm-designate), [STA: Golob to opposition](https://english.sta.si/3547033/pm-golob-announces-his-party-will-be-in-the-opposition), [gov.si: PM Janez Janša](https://www.gov.si/en/state-authorities/prime-minister/janez-jansa/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/22/slovenias-parliament-approves-right-wing-janez-jansa-as-prime-minister) |
