# Script: Dutch election system (59 s, 9:16)

Made to the standing brief "educational motion graphics video, political system" (see `CLAUDE.md`), in the
`GermanElection` look (white background, step header, ballot, one hemicycle, cartoon characters).

**Core message:** how the Dutch choose who runs the country, as one ladder: you vote → parliament (what the House
and the Senate do) → the government (a coalition) → the King, whom nobody votes for.

**Learning goals**
1. How you vote: one vote, a red pencil, one box next to one candidate; seats follow each party's share of the votes.
2. The House of Representatives (150) makes laws, approves the budget, checks the government and can vote it out.
3. The Senate (75) checks every law but can only say yes or no; provincial councils choose it, not voters directly.
4. No party wins a majority (76) alone; in 2025 D66 and the PVV tied at 26, and three parties formed the government.
5. The head of state, King Willem-Alexander, inherited the crown; he signs laws, but the ministers are responsible.

## Fact sheet (checked 13 Oct 2026)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Head of state: King Willem-Alexander, King since 30 April 2013 (Queen Beatrix abdicated). | [royal-house.nl: The Princess of Orange](https://www.royal-house.nl/members-royal-house/the-princess-of-orange) |

| House of Representatives: 150 seats, proportional representation, elected every 4 years or sooner; voters are Dutch nationals aged 18+. Voters mark one candidate with a red pencil. | Senate: 75 members, examines every bill the House has passed and can only adopt or reject it (no right of amendment). | [eerstekamer.nl](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/begrip/english_2), [houseofrepresentatives.nl: Senate](https://www.houseofrepresentatives.nl/how-parliament-works/democracy-netherlands/senate), [How a bill becomes law](https://www.houseofrepresentatives.nl/how-parliament-works/how-bill-becomes-law) |
| Senators are elected by the members of the 12 provincial councils (plus electoral colleges for the Caribbean Netherlands and Dutch voters abroad). Simplified to "provincial councils choose them". | [english.kiesraad.nl: Senate](https://english.kiesraad.nl/elections/elections-of-the-senate) |
| Election of 29 Oct 2025 (official result 7 Nov 2025): D66 26 seats (most votes), PVV 26, VVD 22, GL-PvdA 20, CDA 18, ... 15 parties in total. No party has won a 76-seat majority alone since proportional representation began. | [kiesraad.nl: result 2025](https://www.kiesraad.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/11/7/kiesraad-uitslag-tweede-kamerverkiezing-betrouwbaar), [houseofrepresentatives.nl: groups](https://www.houseofrepresentatives.nl/members_of_parliament/parliamentary_groups) |
