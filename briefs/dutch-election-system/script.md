# Script: Who runs the Netherlands? (Dutch election system, ~44 s, 9:16)

Made to the standing brief "educational motion graphics video, political system" (see `CLAUDE.md`).

**Core message:** The Dutch never vote for their head of state (the King inherits the crown). Power comes
from the elected House of Representatives, and the government is a coalition that the King swears in.

**Learning goals**
1. The head of state is a hereditary monarch: King Willem-Alexander. His daughter Amalia is next in line.
2. The King signs every law, but the ministers carry the responsibility.
3. Voters elect all 150 seats of the House of Representatives (at least every four years).
4. The 75-seat Senate is not elected by the people directly; the provincial councils choose it.
5. 2025 example: no party came close to a majority (76), so parties formed a coalition, sworn in by the King.

**Narrative strategy:** open on a surprising fact ("never vote for their head of state") and a question
("who's in charge?"), then climb from the King, to the voters, to parliament, to the government, and end on
a debate question for the comments. The video stays neutral; only the closing question is meant to polarise.

### Changes from the first approved draft
- The user asked to add the Senate ("say it's not voted for by the people"). With it, the draft ran 52–55 s,
  so the user chose a tighter version (measured with the real voice) that drops the line "Seats follow the vote
  share, so about two-thirds of a percent wins you a seat" and shortens the other sentences.
- "directly" was added to the Senate line for accuracy: people elect the provincial councils, which elect the Senate.
- Closing question chosen by the user: "Is it really democracy if you don't vote for your head of state?"
