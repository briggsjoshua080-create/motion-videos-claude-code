# Script: Danish election system, "Who runs Denmark?" (59.6 s, 9:16)

Made to the standing brief "educational motion graphics video, political system" (see `CLAUDE.md`), in the
`GermanElection` look (white background, step header, ballot card, one hemicycle, cartoon characters), in Denmark's
colours: the Dannebrog's red as the accent, the flag as a Nordic cross bar on the top edge (new `emblem: "nordic"` in
the explainer palette). Real party colours appear only in the 2026 result. Steps in the header:
**Vote · Folketing · Government · King**. Header title **"WHO RUNS DENMARK?"**.

The user chose (15 Oct 2026):
- The hook "Denmark's government has just 82 of 179 seats. And that's normal. Why?"
- The closing question "Should a government rule without a majority behind it?" (it ties back to the hook).
- The narration below as written (approved on 15 Oct 2026).

**Core message:** Danes elect one chamber, the Folketing (179 MPs: 175 in Denmark, 2 each in Greenland and the Faroe
Islands), with one cross for a party or a candidate; parties above 2% share the seats nationwide. In 2026 twelve parties
got in; after three King's rounds and a record 69 days, Mette Frederiksen formed a minority government (82 seats). It
governs because no majority (90) votes against it. The King inherits the throne, signs every law but can't block one.

**Narrative strategy:** the hook is a puzzle (a government without a majority, and that's normal). The story climbs
Vote → Folketing → Government → King and answers the puzzle in the Government step (negative parliamentarism). Neutral
throughout; only the closing question is meant to spark debate.

## Fact sheet (checked 15 Oct 2026)

| **Who can vote:** Danish citizenship, 18+ on election day, permanent residence in the Realm (Denmark, Faroe Islands, Greenland), not under guardianship (Constitution §29). Not compulsory. | [Folketinget: when you can vote](https://www.ft.dk/da/folkestyret/valg-og-afstemninger/hvornaar-man-har-stemmeret), [borger.dk: voting rights](https://www.borger.dk/samfund-og-rettigheder/demokrati-og-valg/Folketingsvalg-og-folkeafstemninger/stemmeret-og-valgbarhed-til-folketingsvalg-og-folkeafstemninger), [valg.im.dk: who can vote](https://www.valg.im.dk/vaelgere/hvem-kan-stemme-og-stille-op-til-valg-i-danmark) |
| **Danes abroad:** as a rule no vote. Exceptions (Election Act §2): posted by a Danish authority or company, working for an international organisation, studying, abroad for health reasons, or intending to return within 2 years; they must apply to be put on the list. | [valg.im.dk: Danes abroad](https://www.valg.im.dk/vaelgere/udlandsdanskeres-valgret), [valg.im.dk: applications](https://www.valg.im.dk/valgmyndigheder/behandling-af-ansoegninger-om-optagelse-paa-folketingsvalglisten) |

| **Ballot:** one cross, next to a party (party vote) or a candidate (personal vote, also counts for the party); more than one cross makes it invalid. | [Folketinget: how to vote](https://www.ft.dk/da/folkestyret/valg-og-afstemninger/hvordan-man-stemmer) |
| **Seats:** 179 MPs: 175 in Denmark (135 constituency seats in 10 multi-member regions + 40 compensatory seats so parties get seats matching their national vote share), 2 Greenland, 2 Faroe Islands. Threshold 2% (Election Act §77), or one constituency seat. Election at least every 4 years. | [Folketinget: number of MPs](https://www.ft.dk/da/ofte-stillede-spoergsmaal/medl_hvor-mange-medlemmer-er-der-af-folketinget), [Folketinget: threshold](https://www.ft.dk/da/ofte-stillede-spoergsmaal/valg_system_hvad-er-spaerregraensen), [valg.im.dk: key figures](https://www.valg.im.dk/noegletal/folketingsvalg) |
| **One chamber:** the Landsting was abolished in 1953; the Folketing passes laws and the budget and controls the government. | [Folketinget: development of the constitution](https://www.ft.dk/da/folkestyret/grundloven-og-folkestyret/grundlovens-og-folkestyrets-udvikling), [Folketinget: control of the government](https://www.ft.dk/da/folkestyret/folketinget/folketingets-kontrol-med-regeringen) |

| **King's rounds:** parties advise the monarch who should lead talks (practice since 1909, not in law). 2026: round 1 (25 Mar) Frederiksen, no government by 8 May; round 2 (8 May) Troels Lund Poulsen (V), no government; round 3 (23 May) Frederiksen; on 1 June she reported a government of S, SF, M and RV could be formed. Ministers appointed 3 June 2026; 69 days of talks, the longest ever. | [Folketinget: what is a King's round](https://www.ft.dk/da/ofte-stillede-spoergsmaal/valg_ftarb_hvad-er-en-kongerunde), [Kongehuset: round III](https://www.kongehuset.dk/presse/pressemeddelelse-kongerunde-iii), [Kongehuset: government formation](https://www.kongehuset.dk/presse/pressemeddelelse-regeringsdannelse-062026), [Statsministeriet: Frederiksen III](https://stm.dk/presse/pressemeddelelser/2026/regeringen-mette-frederiksen-iii/), [DR: new government](https://www.dr.dk/ligetil/indland/danmark-har-faaet-en-ny-regering-2), [UK Commons Library briefing](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10847/) |
| **Minority government:** S 38 + SF 20 + RV 10 + M 14 = 82 of 179 (majority 90). Negative parliamentarism (Constitution §15): a government must go only if a majority votes no confidence; most Danish governments have been minority governments. Opposition parties (V, K, LA, DD, DF, BP): 77. | [Folketinget: §15](https://www.ft.dk/da/dokumenter/bestil-publikationer/publikationer/mingrundlov/min-grundlov/kapitel-3/paragraf-15), [Folketinget: how a government is formed](https://www.ft.dk/da/folkestyret/regeringen/saadan-dannes-en-regering) |
