# Retention plan: "Britain's new PM won under 25,000 votes." (1080×1920, 30 fps, 60.0 s)

Made to the **TikTok Retention Rules** in `CLAUDE.md` and the **comedy formula**
(`briefs/comedy-explainer-formula.md`), in the style of `USMidterms` (the user's request: "same style as US midterms").
Second marks are **measured** from the voice-over (`src/voiceover/burnham-pm.timing.json`: last word ends at 59.0 s,
then a 1.0 s hold). No word-for-word script here; the narration lives in `src/voiceover/burnham-pm.script.json`.

**Premise ("the game"): musical chairs.** To be PM you need a chair (a seat in the Commons). Burnham had none; his party
kept him away from the first free chair; another MP got up and gave him his; then he took the big one. The chair is a
green Commons seat, the running prop.

Loops: **L1** how? (no general election) · **L2** whose seat? · **L3** why now (why did the last PM go)? · **L4** (re-hook 1)
how do you get a seat if you have none? · **L5** (re-hook 2) why would an MP just give his seat away / then what? ·
**L6** (hidden) the small gold crown tag on the chair.
